Privacy Policy

Open WebUI Mobile · Last updated: June 19, 2026

Open WebUI Mobile (“the app”) is a client that connects to a self-hosted Open WebUI server that you configure. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Who controls your data

The app communicates only with the server address you enter. All of your conversations, files, and account data are stored on and processed by that server, which you or your organization operate. The developer of this app does not operate any server, does not receive your data, and has no access to your conversations or account.

Information stored on your device

  • The server address you configure.
  • Your login email, password, and session token, stored securely in the Android Keystore so the app can sign you in automatically. These are used only to authenticate to the server you specified.
  • App preferences such as theme and connection settings.

This information stays on your device and is sent only to your configured server for the purpose of signing in and using the service. It is removed when you sign out or clear the app’s data.

Information sent over the network

The app sends requests only to the server address you configure (typically reached over your private Tailscale network). It does not send data to the app developer or to any third party. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Permissions

  • Internet / network access — to communicate with your server.
  • Camera and microphone — used only when you choose to use the related features (for example, taking a photo or using voice input) inside the embedded web interface. Captured media is sent to your configured server, not to the developer.
  • Photos / files — used only when you choose to attach or upload a file.

These permissions are used solely to enable features you start yourself. The app does not access this data in the background.

Data sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, share, or sell any personal information, because the developer does not receive any of your data.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
